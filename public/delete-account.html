Delete your ChamaOne account

ChamaOne — chama & savings-group manager (app id com.chamaone.app)

Option 1 — in the app (instant)

  1. Open ChamaOne and sign in.
  2. Tap More in the bottom bar.
  3. Under Your account, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — ask us

If you can’t sign in, email privacy@chamaone.app from the email address you registered with, with the subject “Delete my ChamaOne account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email address and password).
  • Your profile: name and phone number.
  • Your membership in every chama, and any pending join requests.
  • Chamas where you are the only member, with all their records.

What is kept, and why

A chama’s ledger is a shared financial record that belongs to the whole group. Entries you took part in (for example a contribution you made or a loan you repaid) stay in that group’s books under your name so its totals remain correct. Your phone number is erased from them and they are no longer linked to any account. They are deleted if the chama itself is deleted. Chamas you created that have other members are handed to the next officer instead of being deleted.
