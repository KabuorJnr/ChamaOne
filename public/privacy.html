ChamaOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

ChamaOne (“the app”, “we”, “us”) helps savings groups (chamas) manage contributions, loans, meetings and records. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. By creating an account or joining a chama you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to run your chama:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and password (stored only as a secure, one-way hash by our authentication provider; we never see your password).
  • Phone number — used to send invite codes and to identify members within a chama.
  • Chama records — the financial and group information you enter: contributions, loans, repayments, meeting notes, motions and votes, and your role in the group.
  • Basic technical data — standard information needed to keep you signed in and to sync your data across devices.

We do not collect payment credentials. ChamaOne does not ask for or store card numbers, bank details, or M-Pesa PINs. Contributions are recorded and confirmed manually by a group officer.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To run your chama — record contributions and loans, hold votes, keep the shared ledger.
  • To sync your group’s data across the phones of its members in real time.
  • To send in-app notifications about activity in your chama.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see your data

Your chama’s records are visible only to the members of that chama. Access is enforced by per-group security rules on our backend — members of one chama cannot see another chama’s data. We do not share your information with third parties except the service providers below, who process it only to run the app.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase, a hosted database and authentication service, and is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Our providers maintain their own security and privacy safeguards.

5. Keeping your data / deleting it

We keep your data for as long as your account and chama are active. You can request that your account and personal data be deleted at any time by contacting us at the address below. When a chama is deleted, its associated records are removed.

6. Children

ChamaOne is intended for adults (18+) managing savings groups and is not directed at children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

8. Contact us

Questions or requests about your data? Email privacy@chamaone.app.
